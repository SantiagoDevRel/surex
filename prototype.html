WHAT SUREX DOES NOT KNOW— the part most products put in the footnotes
1No human audits anything. An open-source model reads the code. Every verdict names the model and prompt version that produced it.
2A review is about a blob, not your machine. Only tier A — a digest match — ties the reviewed bytes to your install. Tier C ties nothing.
3The model is wrong in both directions. It misses things and it flags wrong. Anyone with standing — a person via World ID, an agent via AgentKit — can contest a verdict, and the rebuttal is displayed with equal weight, forever.
4Coverage is partial. 214 of ~3,100 known MCP servers are reviewed — 7%. Absence of a verdict means absence of knowledge, and the UI says so.
5SureX never sees your traffic. The hook queries an index with a fingerprint. What runs on your machine stays unknown to us — which is also why we can't promise it matches what was reviewed.
